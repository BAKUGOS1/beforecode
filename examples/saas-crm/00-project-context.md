# Project Context

## Project Name

MiniCRM

## Raw Idea

Build a lightweight CRM for small service businesses that need to manage inbound leads, assign owners, schedule follow-ups, and track deal progress without the complexity of enterprise CRM software.

## Problem

Small sales teams often manage leads through spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, call notes, and scattered reminders. This causes missed follow-ups, duplicate lead entries, unclear ownership, and weak visibility into the sales pipeline.

## Target Users

- Sales representative who owns daily lead follow-ups
- Sales manager who reviews pipeline health and overdue work
- Admin user who manages team members, sources, and pipeline stages

## Project Type

saas

## MVP Features

- Lead capture with name, phone, email, source, value, owner, and status
- Lead table with search, filters, sorting, and pagination
- Lead detail drawer with notes and activity timeline
- Follow-up scheduling with overdue indicators
- Convert qualified lead into deal
- Deal pipeline with stage movement
- Role-based access for admin, manager, and sales representative

## Out of Scope for v1

- Built-in email sending
- Accounting or invoice generation
- Advanced marketing automation
- Native mobile app
- AI lead scoring
- Marketplace integrations

## Tech Preferences

- JavaScript frontend
- React or Next.js
- Supabase database and authentication
- Row-level security for tenant isolation
- Zod validation for forms

## AI Build Mode

Yes. AI coding agents may implement this project, but they must follow the PRD, TRD, permission matrix, and QA plan before changing code.

## Deadline or Launch Target

MVP in four weeks.
