# SaaS CRM Project Brief

## Project Name

MiniCRM

## One-line Summary

A simple CRM for managing leads, deals, activities, and customers.

## Problem Statement

Small teams often track sales data in spreadsheets, which becomes messy as leads and deals grow.

## Target Users

- Small business owners
- Sales teams
- Freelancers
- Agencies

## Main Goal

Create a lightweight CRM that helps users manage sales workflows clearly.

## Core Features

- Lead management
- Deal pipeline
- Activity tracking
- Search and filters
- Basic dashboard

## MVP Scope

- Create, edit, and view leads
- Convert lead to deal
- Add activities
- View dashboard summary

## Out of Scope

- Billing
- Advanced automation
- Marketplace
- Enterprise reporting

## Success Criteria

- Users can manage leads and deals without spreadsheets
- Core pages load quickly
- Basic workflows are easy to understand
