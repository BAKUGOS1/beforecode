# PRD Review Checklist

## Problem and Outcomes

- [ ] Problem and target users are evidence-backed
- [ ] Vision, principles, goals, metrics, and non-goals agree
- [ ] MVP scope tests a clear hypothesis or outcome
- [ ] Success metrics include baseline, target, and measurement window

## Requirements

- [ ] Requirements use stable IDs and precise language
- [ ] Every must-have has measurable acceptance criteria
- [ ] Business rules, calculations, and lifecycle states are explicit
- [ ] Roles, ownership, and permission behavior are defined
- [ ] Data capture, validation, retention, export, and deletion are covered
- [ ] Integrations include failure and retry expectations

## User Experience

- [ ] Critical journeys include entry, success, and recovery
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, partial, conflict, and offline states are considered
- [ ] Forms preserve data after recoverable errors
- [ ] Responsive and accessibility requirements are measurable
- [ ] Notifications and destructive actions have clear behavior

## Delivery and Risk

- [ ] Dependencies, assumptions, and constraints have owners
- [ ] Security, privacy, operational, and adoption risks are visible
- [ ] Rollout, migration, support, and rollback requirements are included
- [ ] Analytics collect only necessary data and support decisions
- [ ] Open questions do not block technical planning

## Consistency

- [ ] Names, statuses, roles, and fields match UX, TRD, data, API, and QA docs
- [ ] Future scope is not hidden inside MVP requirements
- [ ] No vague terms such as “fast,” “secure,” or “easy” remain without targets
- [ ] Product, engineering, design, and QA owners approve the document
