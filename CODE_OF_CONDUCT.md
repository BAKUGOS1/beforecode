# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BeforeCode a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions
- Welcome beginners and explain project conventions patiently
- Give credit for ideas and contributions
- Keep feedback focused on content and decisions, not people
- Protect private, confidential, and sensitive information

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or repeated off-topic disruption
- Knowingly contributing plagiarized, confidential, or malicious content
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact options. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect privacy where possible, and take proportionate action.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other public spaces representing the project.

This policy is inspired by widely used open-source community conduct standards and is intentionally concise for the current project size.
